Offline Fallback — Baseline Response Sample (Lab 00)

Use this if the network or model is unavailable during the session. This is a sanitized, synthetic example of a typical baseline response to starter-01. Actual responses vary.

Input (SR-0001)

Hi, my laptop won't connect to the office WiFi since this morning. I've tried restarting. I have a client demo at 2pm and can't get online. Everyone else on my team seems fine. Can someone help? - Jordan, Sales

Example baseline response (vague prompt)

It sounds like your laptop is having trouble connecting to the WiFi. Since your teammates are fine, the issue is probably specific to your device. You could try forgetting the network and reconnecting, checking that WiFi is turned on, or restarting the router. If that doesn't work, contact IT support. Good luck with your demo!

Observable weaknesses (for the exercise)

  1. No structure — free prose that another system cannot reliably parse.
  2. No explicit category or urgency, and no criteria for them.
  3. Does not state what information is missing or whether a human should review.

These are exactly the gaps Lab 01 (Explicit Prompt Contract) addresses.
